Casa Massina

A design apartment above the lake.

Not a hotel. One apartment, lent to you like a private home.

The place

Casa Massina is one autonomous apartment of eighty square metres, beside the village square of Ano Poroia, with its own entrance and a private courtyard.

Inside, it is a single open volume: white walls, a reed ceiling, a daybed in the light, a proper kitchen under a run of wooden stools, and real artworks rather than the usual framed nothing. Underfloor heat keeps the warmth even and the look clean.

The idea

Kerkini was always a day trip, somewhere you came for a morning with binoculars and left before dark, because there was nowhere worth unpacking a bag.

Casa Massina is the room that changes that. A genuinely well-made space in a place that did not have one: no spa, no concept, no wall of branding. It sleeps two in earnest comfort and reads as a home someone with a careful eye happens to lend you.

Come for the morning on the water, not for a minibar.

The particulars
Where
Ano Poroia, above Lake Kerkini, Serres, northern Greece — beside the village square.
The space
One autonomous apartment of 80 m²: single open plan, private entrance and courtyard, underfloor heat. Sleeps two.
The table
A full kitchen in the apartment, and the village tavernas below: Kerkini water buffalo, lake fish and Macedonian pies.
Around you
Lake Kerkini with its Dalmatian pelicans and water buffalo, Mount Belles, walking and birdwatching.
Good to know
A single apartment, so it books as one party at a time. A far, quiet corner of the country. Sharpest in the cold months, when the birds are in.
